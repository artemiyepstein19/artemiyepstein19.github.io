Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026 · Last updated: 30 July 2026

Data controller Artemiy Epstein Registered sole proprietor, Serbia info@artemiyepstein.com

1. Scope and controller

This Policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit artemiyepstein.com or submit an inquiry about operations, project management, website development, business software, or related services.

The data controller is Artemiy Epstein, a registered sole proprietor based in Serbia. Privacy requests may be sent to info@artemiyepstein.com.

2. Data collected

The contact forms may collect the information you choose to provide:

  • name and work email;
  • company name and optional company website;
  • the service or product type that interests you;
  • your inquiry, business problem, desired outcome, and other information included in the message;
  • technical data used by the form provider for delivery, security, and spam protection, such as IP address, browser type, submission time, and referring page.

Required fields are marked with an asterisk. Please do not submit special-category or other sensitive personal data through the forms.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

Personal data is processed only as necessary to receive and respond to your inquiry, take steps at your request before a possible service contract, continue relevant business correspondence, protect the website and forms from abuse and spam, meet applicable legal duties, and establish or defend legal claims.

The principal legal grounds are steps requested before entering a contract and the legitimate interest in handling business inquiries and protecting the communication channel. The checkbox confirms that you have read this Policy; it is not consent to marketing, and the site does not contain a newsletter subscription.

4. Service providers and recipients

  • Formspree, Inc. receives contact-form data, supports spam prevention, and forwards the inquiry. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy and security information.
  • Google Workspace is used to receive and handle messages sent to info@artemiyepstein.com. Read the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process standard technical request and security data. Form content is sent by your browser directly to Formspree and is not stored in the public website repository. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Personal data is not sold or shared with third parties for their own direct marketing.

5. International transfers

Some service providers operate outside Serbia or the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are subject to an applicable legal mechanism and safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses, contractual controls, and technical security measures.

6. Retention

Inquiry data is kept only as long as needed to respond and reasonably continue the conversation, normally no longer than 12 months after the last substantive communication. If an inquiry leads to a contract, relevant correspondence and business records may be retained longer when required by Serbian tax, accounting, or other law, or to protect legal claims.

Service providers may keep limited logs, backups, and account data under their own legal duties and retention rules.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable Serbian data-protection law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Send requests to info@artemiyepstein.com. Reasonable identity verification may be required.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used, including HTTPS transmission, access controls, and established service providers. No internet transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Cookies, analytics, AI, and automated decisions

The site currently does not use advertising cookies, marketing trackers, or a web analytics service. Hosting and security providers may process technical request data necessary to provide and protect their services.

Contact-form data is not used for profiling, automated decision-making, model training, or automated AI processing as part of responding to the inquiry.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the website, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will always appear on this page.
